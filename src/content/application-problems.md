# Application Returned? Fixing Common DAR and SAR Proof Problems (Including Supplementals)

::in-short
This guide helps you: find why a DAR or SAR application was questioned, and how to fix it.
Start by: assuming the problem is in the documentation, not the ancestor.
Then: run the [Lineage Evidence Matrix](/tools/lineage-matrix/) report and clear every gap and conflict it lists.
::end

Start by assuming the problem is in the documentation, not the ancestor. DAR has published what it sees most often, and a 2023 Ohio Society SAR presentation listed common SAR problems. This guide turns that guidance into a problem, cause and fix table, then a pre-submission checklist.

## Problem, cause and fix

Points attributed to the 2023 Ohio Society SAR presentation come from that talk, not SAR's national rules. Confirm current requirements with your registrar.

| Problem | Cause the society describes | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Applicant birth certificate questioned | {{asof:sar-apg-bc-gender}} SAR's application guide says "Birth certificate or court order is required for all new applicants that specifies his gender as male." [[claim:sar-apg-bc-gender]] It says "The applicant may need a court order if the birth certificate fails to identify the applicant's gender as male." [[claim:sar-apg-bc-court]] "Short-form birth certificates that do not identify the parents are insufficient." [[claim:sar-apg-bc-shortform]] | The applicant may need a court order if the birth certificate fails to identify the applicant's gender as male. [[claim:sar-apg-bc-court]] Short-form birth certificates that do not identify the parents are insufficient. [[claim:sar-apg-bc-shortform]] |
| Parent-child link questioned | A 2023 Ohio Society SAR presentation said inadequate proof of parent-to-child connections, including unresolved naming conflicts, gets an application pended [[claim:ohsar-naming]] | Add a record that states the relationship; see [Substitute Evidence](/guides/substitute-evidence/) |
| Patriot generation questioned | {{asof:sar-apg-patriot-residence}} SAR's application guide says the patriot's residence during the Revolution must agree with the service being claimed. [[claim:sar-apg-patriot-residence]] {{asof:sar-apg-patriot-age}} SAR's application guide says that if the patriot's birth date can't be found, you cite a proven fact showing the patriot was living at the time of the service and of age for it [[claim:sar-apg-patriot-age]] | Add residence and age evidence for the service years |
| Dates rejected | {{asof:sar-apg-date-format}} SAR's application guide says all dates must be entered in the format 01 Jan 1900 [[claim:sar-apg-date-format]] | Re-enter dates in that format |
| DAR papers used as proof rejected | {{asof:sar-apg-record-copy}} SAR's application guide says a DAR application used as proof must be an official DAR record copy, not a chapter copy [[claim:sar-apg-record-copy]] | Order the DAR record copy |
| Review slowed | A 2023 Ohio Society SAR presentation said superfluous documentation [[claim:ohsar-superfluous]], and lineage data not supported by documents, slow review [[claim:ohsar-unsupported]] | Cut extra papers; delete any data you cannot document |
| Service removed or questioned | DAR: older applications verified when DAR did not require documentation to prove service [[claim:darblog-1890s]] | Find a service source that meets current standards |
| Service questioned | DAR: missing source of service or complete residence [[claim:darblog-missing]] | Add both |
| Service questioned | DAR: residence and service location do not match [[claim:darblog-residence]] | See [Same Name, Different Man](/guides/same-name/) |
| Service questioned | DAR: residence in a known Loyalist center [[claim:darblog-loyalist]] | See [Patriot or Loyalist?](/guides/patriot-or-loyalist/) |
| Service questioned | DAR: it does not recognize the service source [[claim:darblog-unrecognized]] | Supply the original record or a transcription of it |
| Review delayed | DAR: incomplete, incorrect, undocumented or mistyped information [[claim:dar-delay]] | Re-check every field against its document |
| Name missing from certificate | DAR leaves off initials, middle names or maiden names its staff have not verified [[claim:dar-cert-names]] | Document the full name |

## Paper or electronic (DAR)

{{asof:dar-eapp-established}} DAR says its electronic application is only for ancestors already fully established with DAR. [[claim:dar-eapp-established]]
{{asof:dar-paper-analysis}} DAR says a new ancestor, or a lineage that needs an analysis compiling indirect evidence, goes on the paper application. [[claim:dar-paper-analysis]]
{{asof:dar-six-weeks}} DAR says that once the fee is paid and the application is sent for review, staff review generally takes about six weeks. [[claim:dar-six-weeks]]
{{asof:dar-nbm}} DAR says a verified application is then accepted by the National Board of Management at the start of the following month. [[claim:dar-nbm]]

## Supplementals and replacements (SAR)

These points come from SAR's national application checklist, Genealogy Committee Policies and Application Preparation Guide.

- {{asof:sar-checklist-signatures}} SAR's application checklist asks whether the State Registrar, State Secretary and two sponsors have signed a new-member application. [[claim:sar-checklist-signatures]]
- {{asof:sar-checklist-supp-sig}} SAR's application checklist asks whether the member and the State Registrar (or another designated officer) have signed a supplemental application on the back. [[claim:sar-checklist-supp-sig]]
- {{asof:sar-gcp-replace-once}} SAR's genealogy policies say an applicant may replace a pended application with another patriot ancestor one time without paying an additional fee. [[claim:sar-gcp-replace-once]]
- {{asof:sar-gcp-replacements-all-once}} SAR's genealogy policies say replacements were reduced to once for all applications. [[claim:sar-gcp-replacements-all-once]]
- {{asof:sar-gcp-pended-two-years}} SAR's genealogy policies say pended applications stay on file for two years from the date they were pended, then expire and are no longer considered. [[claim:sar-gcp-pended-two-years]]
- {{asof:sar-apg-fee-nonrefundable}} SAR's application guide says an applicant's filing fee is not refundable. [[claim:sar-apg-fee-nonrefundable]] Confirm current fees with your registrar.

## Pre-submission checklist

Points attributed to the 2023 Ohio Society SAR presentation come from that talk, not SAR's national rules. Confirm current requirements with your registrar.

1. Fill the form from the documents, not the documents to the form. A 2023 Ohio Society SAR presentation put it in capitals: always complete an application from the documentation. [[claim:ohsar-from-docs]]
2. Every name, date, place and relationship has a document behind it. [[claim:dar-each-relationship]]
3. {{asof:dar-upload-each}} DAR says every uploaded document is cited, and every cited document is uploaded. [[claim:dar-upload-each]]
4. {{asof:dar-4digit}} DAR says years are four digits. [[claim:dar-4digit]]
5. {{asof:dar-symbols}} DAR says its fields take no slashes, quotation marks or ampersands. [[claim:dar-symbols]]
6. No online family trees as proof. [[claim:dar-online-trees]]
7. Service source is an original record or a transcription of one. [[claim:dar-service-sources]]
8. Run the Lineage Evidence Matrix report and clear every gap and conflict it lists.

A 2023 Ohio Society SAR presentation said a correctly prepared application can be reviewed at National in as little as 30 minutes. [[claim:ohsar-30min]]
