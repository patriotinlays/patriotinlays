# Patriotic Service Without a Musket: Proving Supply Taxes, Oaths, Petitions and Civil Office

::in-short
This guide helps you: prove service off the battlefield: committees, oaths, petitions, town office, supply taxes or furnished goods.
Start by: naming the service type and checking its window in the worksheet's patriot step.
Then: read [Same Name, Different Man](/guides/same-name/) to make sure each record is your patriot's.
::end

Many patriots never carried a musket. They sat on committees, swore oaths, signed petitions, held town office, paid supply taxes or furnished goods. Lineage societies count much of this, but each type has its own evidence and, in a few cases, its own dates.

## The date windows

DAR accepts service performed between 19 April 1775 and 26 November 1783. [[claim:dar-window-core]]
SAR accepts patriotic service rendered between 19 April 1775 and 25 November 1783. [[claim:sar-gcp-window]]
SAR's Step 1 page gives 26 November; this site follows SAR's Genealogy Committee Policies. [[claim:sar-window]] [[claim:sar-gcp-window]]
A few DAR categories have their own windows:

| Service | Window | Source wording |
|---|---|---|
| Committees that furthered the cause of the Colonies | From April 1774 [[claim:dar-committee-1774]] | DAR |
| Virginia Oath of Fidelity | October 1779 to 26 November 1783 [[claim:dar-va-oath]] | DAR |
| Spanish troops under Gálvez or the Louisiana Militia | After 24 December 1776 [[claim:dar-galvez-troops]] | DAR |
| Supplying cattle for Gálvez's forces in Spanish America | After 24 December 1776 [[claim:dar-galvez-cattle]] | DAR |
| Battle of Point Pleasant | 10 October 1774, that location and date only [[claim:sar-point-pleasant]] | SAR |

The patriot step in the free [Lineage Evidence Matrix](/tools/lineage-matrix/) tests your patriot's service dates against these windows for the type of service you pick. DAR does not state an end date for the Gálvez categories, so the worksheet applies the general end of 26 November 1783. [[claim:dar-window-core]]

## Service types and the evidence that fits them

| Service type | How the societies describe it | Records to look for |
|---|---|---|
| Civil office | DAR: civil service under provisional or new state governments [[claim:dar-civil-service]], such as town clerk, selectman, juror, sheriff, constable or justice of the peace [[claim:dar-civil-examples]] | Town meeting minutes, county court orders [[claim:dar-service-sources]] |
| Committees | SAR: committees of safety, observation, inspection and correspondence [[claim:sar-committees]] | Committee minutes, town records |
| Oaths | DAR: signers of oaths of fidelity and allegiance [[claim:dar-oaths]]; SAR adds the Articles of Association and the Association Test [[claim:sar-oaths]] | Oath lists, often in state archives or town records |
| Petitions | DAR: wording has to clearly show loyalty to American independence [[claim:dar-petitions]]; religious petitions do not count [[claim:dar-petitions-religious]] | The full petition text, not just a list of signers |
| Supplies and material aid | DAR: furnishing supplies, lending money, providing munitions [[claim:dar-material-aid]]; SAR adds munitions makers and gunsmiths [[claim:sar-material]] | Receipts, certificates, public claims |
| Supply taxes | DAR treats payment of state supply taxes as patriotic service [[claim:dar-supply-tax]] | Tax lists for qualifying laws (see below) |
| Acting under the new governments | SAR: people directed to hold elections, oversee road work or collect provisions [[claim:sar-directed]] | Court orders and appointments |
| Aid to the wounded | DAR: aid to the wounded other than to one's own immediate family [[claim:dar-aid-wounded]] | Accounts, claims, letters |
| Substitutes | Furnishing a substitute for military service [[claim:dar-substitute]] | Muster records naming the substitute |
| Frontier defense | DAR: documented defenders of the western frontiers [[claim:dar-forts]] | Fort and ranger records |
| Clergy | SAR: overt acts of resistance that only religious leaders could perform [[claim:sar-clergy]] | Sermons, church and town records |
| Virginia land certificates | DAR: applicants for Virginia Certificates of Rights to land for settlement [[claim:dar-va-land]] | Virginia land office records |

## Supply taxes: the detail that decides the claim

Taxes are the most misunderstood category. SAR's Genealogy Committee Policies, §2.4004, now state the current rule: "Payment of a tax to a state or a local jurisdiction during the Revolution shall be deemed Patriotic Service" by virtue of providing material aid, "provided that the taxpayer was not charged a penalty rate or fee imposed for breaking the law and/or the taxpayer was not on record for having broken a law entailing a penalty such as a higher rate, penalty fee, fine, or seizure of property." [[claim:sar-gcp-24004]] [[claim:sar-gcp-24004-penalty]]

The stated exclusions, quoted from §2.4004:

- "A record such as a tax assessment roll, payment list, or a receipt is required." [[claim:sar-gcp-24004-record]]
- "Evidence merely that a person had a legal obligation to pay a tax is not sufficient to establish service." [[claim:sar-gcp-24004-obligation]]
- "Payment of a tax or fee to a state or local jurisdiction shall not be deemed Patriotic Service if the payment was a license or specific governmental service for personal benefit, even if called a tax." [[claim:sar-gcp-24004-license]] "This includes, but is not limited to, marriage licenses, writs, and the inspection or storage of commodities." [[claim:sar-gcp-24004-examples]]

SAR reports apparently no surviving South Carolina or Georgia revolutionary tax lists. [[claim:sartax-scga]]

DAR extended qualifying taxes beyond the Pennsylvania supply taxes in 2006. [[claim:sartax-dar-2006]]
SAR adopted its tax policy in fall 2012. [[claim:sartax-policy-2012]]

## Assemble the proof

1. Name the service type and check its window in the worksheet's patriot step.
2. Find the record that names the ancestor and states the act: the oath, the minute, the receipt, the tax list.
3. Match residence to service. DAR re-examines service when the residence and the service location do not match. [[claim:darblog-residence]]
4. Keep family stories out of it. SAR states that family tradition about an ancestor's service is not proof. [[claim:sar-tradition]] DAR does not take family genealogies as a source of service either. [[claim:dar-no-genealogy-service]]
5. Mark it correctly. On the DAR form, the CS box covers civil service [[claim:dar-cs]] and the PS box covers patriotic service such as supplies, oaths and committees. [[claim:dar-ps]]

DAR reserves the right to decide what service and proof are acceptable. [[claim:dar-reserves-right]]
