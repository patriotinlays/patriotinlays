# Revolutionary War Service Window & Widow-Act Date Checker

Use this checker to see whether the dates you have for an ancestor fall inside the windows that lineage societies and federal pension laws published. It organizes dates; it does not decide eligibility. Everything you type stays in this browser.

::tool service-window-full

## The windows the checker uses

DAR accepts service performed between 19 April 1775 and 26 November 1783. [[claim:dar-window-core]]
SAR accepts patriotic service, with some exceptions, for the same period. [[claim:sar-window]]
Committee service that furthered the cause of the Colonies counts from April 1774. [[claim:dar-committee-1774]]
The Virginia Oath of Fidelity counts from October 1779 to 26 November 1783. [[claim:dar-va-oath]]
Service in the Spanish troops under Gálvez or the Louisiana Militia counts after 24 December 1776. [[claim:dar-galvez-troops]]
Supplying cattle for Gálvez's forces in Spanish America counts after 24 December 1776. [[claim:dar-galvez-cattle]]
DAR gives no end date for the two Gálvez categories or for committee service, so the checker applies the general end date and says so in its result.

## Widow pension and bounty-land laws

The 1832 act covered veterans with at least 6 months of service, with full pay for life at 2 years or more. [[claim:nara-act-1832]]
It covered service in the Continental Line or State troops, volunteers or militia. [[claim:nara-act-1832-categories]]
The 1836 act required the widow to have married the veteran before his last period of service expired. [[claim:nara-act-1836]]
The checker tests the 1836 marriage date and months of service only; it does not evaluate the 1832 service-category rules the 1836 act inherits. [[claim:nara-act-1832-categories]]
The 1838 act granted 5-year pensions to widows married before January 1, 1794. [[claim:nara-act-1838]]
The July 1848 act granted life pensions to widows married before January 2, 1800. [[claim:nara-act-1848]]
Acts in 1853 and 1855 removed the marriage-date restrictions. [[claim:nara-act-1853-1855]]
The source used here gives no other terms for those two acts, so the checker reports them as not evaluated.
The 1878 act covered widows of soldiers who served as few as 14 days or were in any engagement. [[claim:nara-act-1878]]
The March 1855 bounty-land act covered veterans with as few as 14 days of service, and their widows and minor children. [[claim:nara-bounty-1855]]

## What a result means

A green result reads: "Date range appears consistent with published rule; verify service category and primary evidence." It is a starting point for research, not a ruling. "Not evaluated" means the published source does not give enough detail to test that rule.
