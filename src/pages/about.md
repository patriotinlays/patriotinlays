# About Patriot In Lay Terms

Patriot In Lay Terms (patriotinlays.com) helps people prove their family line back to someone who supported American independence, usually for an application to the Daughters of the American Revolution (DAR) or the Sons of the American Revolution (SAR). It's written for the person who has never done genealogy before, and it tries to stay useful for people who have.

## What you can do here

The site has ten plain-English guides and two free tools that run in your browser. The guides explain the records you're likely to meet, such as pension files, bounty-land warrants and censuses, what each one can show, and what to try when a record is missing. The Lineage Evidence Matrix lets you list each generation from you back to the patriot and gives you a to-do list of the records still missing, with where to look. The Widow Pension Law Checker shows which federal widow pension laws a couple's marriage fits, so you can decide whether to look for her pension file. Together they list what you have, what you still need, and where to look next.

## What this site doesn't do

It isn't affiliated with DAR, SAR, The National Society of the Colonial Dames of America (NSCDA), the National Archives or any other organization it cites. It doesn't decide anyone's membership; only a society's own genealogists do that. It doesn't search records for you, and it isn't legal or professional genealogical advice.

## How the facts are kept accurate

Every factual statement on the site is tied to a quoted passage from an official or institutional source, such as the National Archives, the National Park Service, the Library of Congress, DAR, SAR or a state library. Each time the site is updated, and on a regular schedule, software checks that each quoted passage still appears in its source. If a statement can't be matched to a source, it's left out. Each page lists its sources with links at the bottom, and the full method is on the [Sources & Methodology](/sources/) page.

## Corrections and suggestions

If you find an error, or have an idea for a tool or guide that would help, please tell us through the [Contact page](/contact/). Each correction is checked against the cited source before the page is updated. Every suggestion is read, though not every one can be added.
