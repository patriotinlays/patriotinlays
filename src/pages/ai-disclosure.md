# AI disclosure

## How AI is used

Written with AI assistance and reviewed by the site's owner. Each fact is tied to a quoted official or institutional source, and software checks that the quote is still there. The owner supervises the project, decides what goes on the site and reads the pages; the source check is what holds every fact to its quote.
