# Privacy

## What you type stays in your browser

The Widow Pension Law Checker and the Lineage Evidence Matrix save what you type only in your own web browser, on the device you're using. Nothing you type is sent to this site or to anyone else. That means your work is still there when you come back on the same device and browser, but it won't follow you to a different computer or phone.

## Anyone using the same browser can see it

Because the tools save your work in the browser, anyone who uses the same device and browser could see what you entered. Private or incognito windows usually throw it away when you close them. Please don't type Social Security numbers, account numbers, passwords, medical details, or private details about living people. Names, rough dates and short notes about your records are all the tools need.

## How to delete it

Each tool has its own button for this. On the Widow Pension Law Checker, press "Clear the form" or "Delete my saved checker data". On the Lineage Evidence Matrix, press "Delete all my worksheet data". Each button removes only its own tool's saved work. You can also delete this site's data in your browser's settings: look for "site data" or "website data" and remove patriotinlays.com. Clearing your browser's history and site data for all sites removes it too.

## What the site itself collects

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, so Cloudflare's servers deliver every page you load.

On pages that show an email address, Cloudflare adds a small script that hides the address from spam robots.

If a page fails to load, your browser may send Cloudflare a short network-error report.

There are no ads at present. If advertising is ever added, this page will be updated first to describe the advertising provider, its use of cookies, and how to opt out.

## Questions

Questions about privacy can be sent to contact@patriotinlays.com (see the [Contact](/contact/) page).

## Technical note

The tools use the browser's local storage, which is not encrypted. The Widow Pension Law Checker's saved items begin with "pi.toolA" and the Lineage Evidence Matrix's begin with "pi.toolB".
