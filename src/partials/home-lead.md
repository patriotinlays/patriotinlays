## Start here: the Widow Pension Law Checker

**What it tells you:** type a widow's marriage date and what you know about her husband's Revolutionary War service, and see, law by law, which federal widow pension and bounty-land laws from 1836 to 1878 her dates fit. [[claim:nara-act-1836]]

**Why it matters:** widows could apply for pensions beginning in 1836. [[claim:nara-amrev-1836]] A widow's application may give her maiden name and the date and place of the marriage, so her file can prove a link in your line. [[claim:m804-widow-info]] The checker shows which laws her dates fit, explains each result, and links to where the pension files are. [[claim:nara-amrev-familysearch]]
