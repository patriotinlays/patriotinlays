**What it does.** You type the marriage date and what you know about his service. It tests them against the date rules in each federal widow pension and bounty-land law, as the National Archives describes them, and shows the result law by law.

**What it doesn't do.** It does not look anything up, and it never makes a decision about membership or a pension; your society does that. It never says a law simply fits, because every law has terms it can't test from dates, and it lists them.

**Saved only here.** Everything you type stays in this browser. A rough date can leave a law "Still possible", and "About" is this site's rule of thumb, not part of the law.
