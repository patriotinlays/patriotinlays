## Words you'll see here

- **Patriot:** someone who supported the cause of American independence, for example by fighting in the battles of the Revolution, serving in the Continental Congress or signing the Declaration of Independence. [[claim:sar-about-patriots]]
- **DAR:** the Daughters of the American Revolution, open to any woman 18 or older who can prove she descends from an ancestor who aided in achieving American independence. [[claim:dar-name]] [[claim:dar-join-eligible]]
- **SAR:** the National Society of the Sons of the American Revolution, open to any man 18 or older who can prove he descends from a patriot of the American Revolution. [[claim:sar-name]] [[claim:sar-about-age]] [[claim:sar-about-eligible]]
- **NSCDA:** The National Society of the Colonial Dames of America, whose members descend from men and women who served the American colonies before July 5, 1776. [[claim:nscda-name-national]] [[claim:nscda-members-colonial]]
- **Lineage society:** a group whose membership is based on lineage, meaning you descend from an ancestor the society accepts. [[claim:nscdama-lineage]] [[claim:dar-join-eligible]] [[claim:sar-about-eligible]] DAR and SAR require you to document that descent; NSCDA is another example. [[claim:dar-join-docs]] [[claim:sar-bloodline]] [[claim:nscdama-lineage]] On this site, "society" on its own means a lineage society.
- **Registrar:** the person in a local chapter who helps you apply. At SAR, your local contact is often the chapter registrar, who guides you through the application, can review what you've gathered, and receives the finished application to begin processing. [[claim:sar-sponsor-registrar]] [[claim:sar-registrar-guide]] [[claim:sar-registrar-process]]
- **Bounty land:** free public land. A bounty-land warrant was a right to free land in the public domain, granted to veterans and their heirs for their service. [[claim:m804-bl-define]] [[claim:m804-bl-who]]
- **Stat.:** short for the Statutes at Large, the published federal laws. A citation such as "5 Stat. 128" gives the volume number, then the page in that volume. [[claim:loc-stat-name]] [[claim:loc-stat-volume]] [[claim:loc-stat-page]]
