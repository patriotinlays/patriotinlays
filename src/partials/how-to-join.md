## How to join, in short

1. **Check that you can join.** DAR is open to any woman 18 or older who can prove she descends from an ancestor who aided in achieving American independence. [[claim:dar-join-eligible]] SAR is open to any man 18 or older who can prove he descends from a patriot of the American Revolution. [[claim:sar-about-age]] [[claim:sar-about-eligible]] (boys under 18 can apply for junior membership) [[claim:sar-apg-junior]] (A **patriot** here means someone who supported the cause of American independence, for example by fighting in its battles or serving in the Continental Congress.) [[claim:sar-about-patriots]]
2. **Find local help.** DAR's [chapter locator](https://www.dar.org/discover/become-member/chapter-locator) finds a chapter near you, and its Membership Interest Form connects you to one. [[claim:dar-join-locator]] [[claim:dar-join-interest]] For SAR, the next step is finding a sponsor near you; [find your state's contact](https://www.sar.org/find-your-contact/). [[claim:sar-sponsor-registrar]]
3. **Gather what you know, then the records.** DAR asks for documentation of each birth, marriage and death in your line, and of your patriot's service. [[claim:dar-join-docs]]
4. **Apply with help.** A DAR member helps guide you through completing and submitting the application. [[claim:dar-member-guides]] An SAR application, with its documentation, goes to your sponsoring chapter for approval. [[claim:sar-apply-submit]]

Official pages: [DAR: How to Join](https://www.dar.org/discover/become-member/how-join) · [SAR: Apply in 4 Easy Steps](https://www.sar.org/how-to-apply/)
