## What to do next

1. List the records you have for each generation in the free [Lineage Evidence Matrix](/tools/lineage-matrix/). It gives you a to-do list of what is missing, where to look, and a plan you can print.
2. Check your patriot's service dates with the free [Service Window Checker](/tools/service-window-checker/).
3. Find a local chapter. DAR's [Chapter Locator](https://www.dar.org/discover/become-member/chapter-locator) finds DAR chapters in your area. [[claim:dar-chapter-locator]] For SAR, your sponsor is often the local chapter registrar; [pick your state on SAR's contact page](https://www.sar.org/find-your-contact/). [[claim:sar-sponsor-registrar]]
4. Start the application with that person's help: a DAR member, or your SAR sponsor, helps guide you through it. [[claim:dar-member-guides]] [[claim:sar-sponsor-guides]]
