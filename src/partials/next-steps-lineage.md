## What to do next

1. Record the patriot and each generation in the free [Lineage Evidence Matrix](/tools/lineage-matrix/). It gives you a to-do list of what is missing and where to look, fitted to each person's era and state, and a plan you can print.
2. If a widow applied for a pension, check which widow pension laws fit with the free [Widow Pension Law Checker](/tools/widow-pension-checker/).
3. Find a local chapter. DAR's [Chapter Locator](https://www.dar.org/discover/become-member/chapter-locator) finds DAR chapters in your area. [[claim:dar-chapter-locator]] For SAR, the next step is finding a sponsor near you; [pick your state on SAR's contact page](https://www.sar.org/find-your-contact/). [[claim:sar-sponsor-registrar]]
4. Start the application with that person's help: a DAR member, or your SAR sponsor, helps guide you through it. [[claim:dar-member-guides]] [[claim:sar-sponsor-guides]]
