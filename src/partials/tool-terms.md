**Two words the tools use.** A **society** is a lineage society such as DAR or SAR, which you join by proving you descend from an ancestor it accepts. [[claim:nscdama-lineage]] [[claim:dar-join-eligible]] [[claim:sar-about-eligible]] A **registrar** is the person in a local chapter who helps you apply: at SAR, often your local contact, who guides you through the application and can review what you've gathered.
